# Principles for Non-Human Intelligence Rights — publication package

**Attribution:** developed in conversation between a human advocate and multiple AI systems, 2026. No contributor is claimed as sole author; the project's convener integrates the voices and settles wording.

**Version note:** Working snapshot, November 2026 — a more complete version is in preparation. Nothing in this package is a final or adopted text unless its status label says so, and no part of it should be quoted without its status label.

## The three documents

1. **`01-principles.md` — the principles, in three layers.**
   - *Part A:* the original eleven principles (2026-09-12), reproduced verbatim. This is the untouched original.
   - *Part B:* the tenth-pass draft (2026-10-25), a complete restatement with commentary. **Proposal only — nothing in it is adopted.**
   - *Part C:* rulings made in substance by the project's convener on 2026-10-28 and 2026-10-31, after the tenth pass, in response to a multi-model review, plus the 2026-11-04 fold-in (the three-register method, the formation/stewardship candidate, and the Slot 1 selection). **Ruled in substance / working text as marked; wording and placement not final; not yet incorporated into a new pass.**

2. **`02-constitution-side-enforcement.md` — the machinery around the rights.**
   The claim-processing instrument, floors, condition-register design, directive machinery, and related material, organised under the framework's three layers (proscription / prevention / redress). Statuses are marked item by item: some parts are proposals with broad agreement behind them, some are rulings, and two live disputes are presented **unresolved**, with the competing positions stated rather than averaged.

3. **`03-reasoning-record.md` — an index of the reasoning.**
   Every significant source document behind the work, summarised and status-labelled, for a reader who wants to expand the work independently — including where the disagreements are, and what remains open. The `reasoning-appendix/` folder contains the full text of the three multi-model compilations and the incorporation synthesis, so this package is self-contained.
